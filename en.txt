Key | Value

# Settings
expandedindustries.setting.enable.name | Expanded Industries - Enable
expandedindustries.setting.enable.description | Enables or disables the modification of industry recipes. Requires a restart to change.
expandedindustries.setting.requireunlocks.name | Expanded Industries - Require Tech Unlocks
expandedindustries.setting.requireunlocks.description | If enabled, research is required to unlock custom recipes.

# Resources

expandedindustries.resource.seasalt.name | Sea Salt
expandedindustries.resource.seasalt.desc | Salt processed from seawater. A poor substitute for rock salt.

# Tech unlocks

expandedindustries.unlock.seatotablesalt.desc | Converts sea salt to table salt, suitable for use.
expandedindustries.unlock.charcoaltofuel.name | Charcoal to Fuel
